About Family Camp

Family Camp is a wonderful gathering of the family of God, with a particular focus on families with young children through elementary school age.

Family Camp is for the entire family! From the youngest to the oldest, everyone at Family Camp can be freshly impressed with the preciousness of God, the Bible, and His people. The goal of Family Camp is not only to strengthen each family's pursuit of Christ, but also to build up the church, the household of God (Ephesians 2:19-20).
