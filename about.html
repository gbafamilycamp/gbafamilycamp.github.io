About Family Camp

Family Camp is a wonderful gathering of the family of God, with a particular focus on young children through elementary school age. While the children are infused with the preciousness of the Bible, proper human character, and the family of God, the parents are encouraged through mutual fellowship with one another.

What to Expect

Family Camp is filled with joyful singing, engaging story time, hands-on crafts, fun activities, snack time, and more! There is also a toddlers play area as well as a nursing mother's room.
