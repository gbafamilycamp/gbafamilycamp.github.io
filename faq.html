Frequently Asked Questions

I'm in junior high or high school. Can I still participate?

Yes! Your participation at family camp is very important! Register as a serving one and take the opportunity to serve those younger than you!

I want to join, but I'm not sure that I can commit to full-time attendance. Should I still register?

Yes! There is an option for part-time attendance on the registration form.

Can I register on site?

On-site registration is possible, but please register before the deadline so we can guarantee that your child will have access to snack, craft materials, etc.

I'd like to bring my neighbor and their family. Is that okay?

Yes! Friends and neighbors are always welcome. Please send them the registration form so we can prepare for them.

I would like to visit, but don't have a family of my own to bring along. How should I register?

You're still welcome to come! Please register as a visitor or as a serving one. The registration team will be in touch with you about how you can best participate.

I'm not from the Boston area. Can I still come and participate?

Of course! Register here.
